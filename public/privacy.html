Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Red Point Elite ("the Extension") is built with your privacy as a default. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Nothing sent to our servers. All reading data (text, position, settings, streak) is stored locally in your browser via localStorage and chrome.storage.sync. It never leaves your device.
  • License key. If you upgrade to Pro, your license key is stored in chrome.storage.sync (synced across your Chrome profile by Google). We do not store it on our servers.
  • Payment data. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

What we do NOT collect

  • The text you read
  • Which websites you visit
  • Your browsing history
  • Any personally identifiable information
  • Analytics or telemetry of any kind

Third-party services

The Extension uses Stripe for payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to any payment interactions.

Permissions

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab — to read the current page's text when you click the icon.
  • scripting — to inject the reader overlay into the page.
  • contextMenus — to add the right-click "Read with Red Point" option.
  • storage — to save your settings and license key.
